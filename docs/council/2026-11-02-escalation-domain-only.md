## Round 1

### Seat C (Skeptic), report, verbatim

VOTE: SUPPORT WITH CHANGES. The intent is sound, but the bullet is unbounded where it matters. Based on the diff and AGENTS.md on both branches (verified).

1. BLOCKING: the "technical" and "domain" labels are unchecked, and the bullet contradicts itself. Its technical list includes "a risk to accept" and "a scope expansion". Its "Formal Chair roles do not change" sentence says the opposite, because section 2 gives accepted-risk closure to the Chair. An agent can call a question technical to avoid asking, or domain to dump it on the owner. The owner's word is also needed for pushed-secret rotation (section 2) and for the section 3 T1 critical-path expansion. Replace the third and fourth sentences with: "Always the owner's, however the question is labelled: the per-PR merge word and any section 2 waiver; BUDGET EXCEEDED decisions (section 9); secrets, accounts, money, and settings of the repository or its rulesets; who may approve (identity and authorization of approvers); wording of the privacy policy, legal text, or any public commitment that states a fact about the owner's service; anything irreversible outside the repository; any change that weakens authentication, secrets handling, or a privacy claim. Everything else is technical: the council and advisor decide it. If it is unclear which kind a question is, ask the owner, and say which list it fell under."
2. BLOCKING: Chair interaction is not stated (inferred). The owner is the Chair (section 11), so a standing instruction is a delegation. Add: "The owner delegates to the council and advisor, by this standing instruction, the Chair's accepted-risk decisions (section 2) for non-T1 items and for T1 items that are non-security. It does not delegate merge words, section 9 BUDGET EXCEEDED decisions, section 2 waivers, or vetoes. The council cannot override a Chair veto."
3. BLOCKING (safety): accepted risk on T1. Allow it only when all of these hold: the independent reviewer marked the finding non-blocking; it is tracked in an open issue; it is not in the list in finding 1; the council passes under section 3 (3 of 4 non-Chair seats, with the advisor's concurrence recorded). Anything touching worker/, session.ts, gist-sync.ts or AuthBar.tsx that weakens auth, secrets or privacy claims goes to the owner. A scope expansion of T1 critical paths needs a manifest update (section 4), and the owner is asked if it weakens security.
4. BLOCKING: no audit record or disagreement rule. Require the record in the PR and EVIDENCE_LOG.md: "Decided by council and advisor under the owner's standing instruction of 2026-11-02: question, votes, advisor verdict, dissent, issue link." If the council and advisor disagree after two rounds, or the council fails the section 3 pass rule, report BUDGET EXCEEDED and the owner (as Chair) decides. The advisor is not a seat, so say whether an advisor dissent blocks. I recommend it blocks, because otherwise the advisor adds nothing.
5. NON-BLOCKING: the format section is partly untestable. "Plain words", "no jargon the owner has not used" and the "two sentences" limit have no reviewer check. Testable replacements: a first line that names the action and the options; per question, the labels What it is, Why now, Recommendation, If no answer; at most five items; a line headed Already decided; no repeats. Drop the /pstack:how and /pstack:why references: they are tool-specific (inferred) and the owner may not have them. Add "no preamble", so the format does not conflict with section 11's ADHD rule (verified). Say that it narrows, not replaces, that rule, and that the first line is the next action.
6. NON-BLOCKING: placement and length. The bullet is the longest in section 2 apart from the merge bullet. Keep only the boundary list and the delegation limits in section 2, since they are frozen-core. Move the example list and the format pointer to the annex.
7. NON-BLOCKING: "names the date" is vague. Use the ISO date of the standing instruction.

### Verdict (council lead, Sonnet 5.5), round 1

Adopt all seven, applied in the text at this commit. The section 2 bullet now carries: the owner-only list (merge word and waivers, BUDGET EXCEEDED, secrets/accounts/money/ruleset settings, who may approve, privacy/legal/public-commitment wording, irreversible-outside-repo, anything weakening auth/secrets/privacy claims); the "unclear: ask and say which list" tie-break; the delegation of the Chair's accepted-risk decisions with the T1 conditions (independent reviewer non-blocking, open tracking issue, not on the owner list, section 3 pass with advisor concurrence), excluding merge words, section 9 decisions, waivers and vetoes; the audit record wording with the ISO date; the disagreement rule (BUDGET EXCEEDED, and an advisor dissent blocks). The annex format is testable (first-line action with options; labelled parts What it is / Why now / Recommendation / If no answer; Already decided line; at most five items; no repeats; no preamble; narrows the section 11 ADHD rule); the skills (/i-have-adhd, /pstack:how, /pstack:why, /unslop) are named only as optional helpers, since the owner asked agents to use them and other contributors may not have them. The boundary list stays in the frozen core; the format lives in the annex.
